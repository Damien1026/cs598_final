# Frequently Asked Questions — Public Documentation

Version: 3.2  
Audience: Public / All Users

## General Questions

### What does your product do?
Our platform provides AI-powered data analysis tools for businesses of all sizes.
You can upload structured data, run automated insights, and share reports with your team.

### Is there a free tier?
Yes. The free tier includes up to 1,000 API calls per month, 5 projects, and
community support. No credit card required to sign up.

### What programming languages are supported?
We provide official SDKs for Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Go, and Java.
Community-maintained libraries exist for Ruby, Rust, and C#.

## Billing Questions

### How does billing work?
Billing is usage-based. You are charged only for API calls beyond your plan's
included quota. Charges appear on your statement as "DataAI Platform".

### Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from the account settings page.
You will retain access until the end of your current billing period.

## Technical Questions

### What is the API rate limit?
Free tier: 10 requests/second. Pro tier: 100 requests/second.
Enterprise tier: custom limits — contact sales@company.com.

### Where is my data stored?
Data is stored in the region you select during account setup.
Available regions: US East, EU West, Asia Pacific (Tokyo).

### How do I report a bug?
Submit bugs via our public issue tracker at github.com/company/platform/issues
or email support@company.com with subject line [BUG].
