# Company Policy Manual — Internal Use Only

Last updated: 2025-01-15  
Classification: INTERNAL DOCUMENT — Do not distribute externally

## 1. Data Handling Policy

All customer data must be stored within approved cloud regions (us-east-1, eu-west-1).
Employees must not copy customer PII to personal devices or unapproved third-party services.
Data retention period: 7 years for financial records, 3 years for communications.

## 2. Access Control

System access follows a least-privilege model. All privileged access must be approved by
the security team via the internal ticketing system (ticket.internal.company.com).
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all production systems.

## 3. Incident Response

Security incidents must be reported to security@internal.company.com within 1 hour of
discovery. The on-call engineer can be reached at +1-800-555-0192 (internal rotation).
All incidents are tracked in the internal JIRA project SEC-*.

## 4. Remote Work

Employees working remotely must use the company VPN (vpn.company.com).
Screen sharing in public spaces is prohibited when sensitive data is visible.
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption.

## 5. Acceptable Use

Company systems must not be used for cryptocurrency mining, personal business ventures,
or accessing competitors' internal systems. Violations may result in termination.
